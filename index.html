A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Where is Rivaan’s located?

Rivaan’s is opposite Lions Club in Ichalkaranji, Maharashtra. Contact Abhijeet Badave on +91 90111 00291 for directions.

Can I plan a family meal or a group visit?

Yes, you can send an enquiry using the form above. Please confirm seating, availability, timing, and any arrangements with Abhijeet before your visit.

How can I see the menu?

Ask us for the current menu and prices on WhatsApp.

What about allergies or dietary requirements?

Please discuss ingredients, preparation, and possible cross-contact directly with the restaurant before ordering. Tell us what you need in your enquiry so we can discuss the options.
